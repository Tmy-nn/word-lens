How it works & limitations

Try the sample photo or open your camera, then tap a detection box or an object name. Change the word language and press the speaker button to hear it. The sample uses the real detector, not preset boxes.

A selected word stays visible when its object leaves the frame. Tracking may confuse objects of the same type. This detects object categories; it does not translate text or identify specific products.

Pronunciation uses your browser's speech voices. Availability and sound vary by device. Some voices use an online speech service. Object words are provided by a curated vocabulary, not generated translations.
